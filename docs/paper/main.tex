\begin{abstract}
Internet operators rely on BGP monitoring platforms to detect routing
anomalies, yet these platforms produce thousands of daily alerts while
few are confirmed as actionable. Triage by static thresholds and
heuristic rules cannot separate a traffic disturbance caused by a
routing change from one that merely coincides with it. We present BGP
Tracer, a two-phase attribution system over RIPE RIS routing telemetry
and Cloudflare Radar traffic aggregates. Phase~1 runs detectors on a
shared five-minute grid and returns a categorical verdict that filters
benign events. Phase~2 applies a paired displacement, difference-in-differences
test (\textsc{paddle}): the data plane's trough onset anchors the
measurement, the control plane is compared against the same clock slots on
the preceding days, and a direction is committed only if four vetoes
pass---precedence against a simultaneous shock, exclusivity against the
same-clock placebo null, provenance scope, and directional congruence.
Every refusal returns the veto that failed and the
statistic behind it. Two consequences are measured. First, ordering by
onset against a same-clock null instead of point estimates removes the
dominant failure mode: on a simultaneous common shock, false directional
commitments fall to $2.0\%$--$6.0\%$ of $400$ windows,
while planted mechanisms are recovered with $100\%$ precision in all
twelve lag--magnitude cells ($2{,}342$ commitments, none wrong).
Second, the test is conservative at independence ($0.0\%$ rejections at
$\alpha=0.05$ across three autocorrelation regimes) and stays near
nominal under serial dependence, which we report rather than
round to nominal. On the public corpus now in the tree the primary
result is a coverage result: $121$ of $280$ cached windows carry no
traffic observation at all, and of the $159$ that can be scored only $3$
commit a direction---rising to $18$ when the exclusivity level is raised
to $0.99$, so the additional $15$ are commitments the placebo null does
not support. Each stop returns
its reason, so the refusals form an auditable record of what was missing
from the evidence rather than a silent decline.



\vspace{1ex}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} BGP routing, anomaly detection, causal inference, root-cause analysis, traffic attribution, network dependability.
\end{abstract}
