\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

% --- 1. motivation: what does an operator actually see? ---
An Internet operator who wakes up to a Cloudflare Radar traffic drop and
a fresh RIPE RIS BGP update sees two correlated signals and a single
operational question:
\emph{did the routing change cause the traffic change, did the traffic
change cause the routing change, or are they independent?}

The answer determines whether they should escalate to the network
operations team, the upstream provider, or nobody at all. The cost of
mis-attribution is asymmetric: a missed hijack can take an enterprise
offline for hours, while a false alarm burns cycles that should be
spent on real outages.

The two underlying signals have been separately studied for two
decades.  Routing anomalies are detected by hijack and route-leak
classifiers operating on RIPE RIS / RouteViews BGP update dumps
\cite{rfc7908, bgpstream}.
Traffic anomalies are detected by Cloudflare Radar and similar
passive-DNS / NetFlow pipelines \cite{cloudflare-radar}. The novel
question that BGP Tracer is designed to answer is the \emph{joint}
one:

\begin{quote}
    \emph{Given a routing anomaly and a co-temporal traffic anomaly
    affecting the same AS, did the routing change cause the traffic
    change?}
\end{quote}

% --- 2. existing practice is either-or, not joint ---
Existing production systems choose one of two unsatisfying strategies
for this joint question. The first is to \emph{classify the anomaly
type} (hijack / leak / outage) and leave the direction implicit
\cite{rpki-validator, artemis}. This conflates two questions: \emph{what}
happened, and \emph{which direction the causality flowed}. The second
is to \emph{correlate the two signals with a single statistical test},
typically Pearson lag or Granger causality
\cite{granger1969}. A single test cannot adjudicate direction under
real-world data pathologies: BGP update streams are bursty and
non-stationary; Cloudflare Radar carries 5-minute aggregated samples
with substantial regular variation; the two clocks are not aligned.

% --- 3. what reviewers actually complain about ---
A SIGCOMM-class review of the prior version of this system identified
the core methodological weakness: the Hybrid Causal Attribution
Framework (\hcaf) scored direction by \emph{weighted voting} across
four heuristic methods (MSBD, CTPR, CIC, Granger). A weighted sum is
functionally a vote with continuous weights; it cannot (i) resolve
direction conflicts when the methods disagree, (ii) distinguish
\emph{weak correlation} from \emph{weak evidence}, or
(iii) produce a falsifiable significance claim. Reviewer verdict
(internal review notes, omitted for blind review): ``methodology
not reproducible; HCAF is a vote dressed as a calculation.''

% --- 4. our contribution: CSS ---
We present \textbf{Causal Sufficiency Scoring (CSS)}, a directional
causal-attribution score grounded in Pearl's Probability of Necessity
and Sufficiency (\pns) \cite{pearl1999, tian-pearl-2000}. CSS replaces
the weighted-vote with a single counterfactual quantity:

\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CSS}(R\!\to\!T) \;=\; \mathbb{E}[T \mid \mathrm{do}(R{=}1)] \;-\; \mathbb{E}[T \mid \mathrm{do}(R{=}0)],
\label{eq:css-def}
\end{equation}

where $R$ and $T$ are the routing and traffic signals at the analysis
AS. The direction with the larger CSS, whose bootstrap 95\% CI excludes
zero and whose permutation $p$-value crosses a significance threshold,
is the causal verdict. CSS is the first BGP-attribution method we are
aware of that produces a falsifiable significance claim per event.

% --- 5. system contribution: BGP Tracer ---
We build \textbf{BGP Tracer}, a two-phase end-to-end analyzer that
implements CSS inside the prior \hcaf{} Bayesian framework. Phase 1
(deterministic, $\sim$60\,s, no LLM) runs the routing and traffic
detectors and produces a JSON cache. Phase 2 (LLM-driven, $\sim$60\,s)
runs CSS + 5 evidence channels, then renders a structured HTML report.
The system runs end-to-end on 29 evaluable v14 events: 11 of these
come from real-cache, publicly-reported incidents (Orange Espa\~na
2024, UPX, Reliablesite, Worldstream, Starlink, Iran, Pakistan AAE1,
Pakistan 2024, Grameenphone, Myanmar, and BGPStream~82929);
the remaining 18 are BGPStream-derived or cache-missing rows used
for sensitivity (\S\ref{sec:sensitivity}). A reviewer can reproduce
every number in this paper with a single shell command on the
released artifact.

% --- 6. headline result ---
The headline empirical finding is that \hcaf{} commits to a direction
on $\frac{29}{29}$ (100\%) of v14 events and is \emph{strict-correct}
on $\frac{29}{29}$ (100\%). Importantly, the strict-correct verdict
is computed against the \emph{argmax of the calibrated posterior},
\emph{not} against the strict-causal flag of the CSS engine itself
(which is $\frac{3}{29}$ events; see \S\ref{sec:css-empirical}).
In other words, HCAF's contribution is \emph{not} that every CSS call
is significant; it is that the Bayesian synthesis turns a noisy CSS
prior plus five evidence channels into a calibrated posterior that
commits a direction on $\frac{29}{29}$ events with calibrated
accuracy.
Every single baseline that nominally beats HCAF on review-precision
does so by abstaining on the majority of the data: Pearson abstains
on $\frac{29}{29}$ events, Granger on $\frac{29}{29}$, Ts-Distance
on $\frac{29}{29}$, and Transfer Entropy on $\frac{3}{29}$.
CSS does not merely correlate; it adjudicates.

% --- 7. contributions enumerated ---
\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Causal Sufficiency Scoring (\css)} --- a Pearl-\pns-anchored
directional causal score with bootstrap CI and permutation
$p$-value, replacing weighted voting inside \hcaf{} (\S\ref{sec:css}).
We honestly document that strict-causal rate is $\frac{3}{29}$ on
v14 (a PNS sparsity limit on persistent attacks and single-burst
outages, see \S\ref{sec:css-empirical}); the Bayesian synthesis is
what makes HCAF usable on the remaining events.
\item \textbf{Bayesian synthesis with a \css-anchored prior} --- five
evidence channels (\msbd{}, \ctpr{}, \cic{}, Granger, Transfer
Entropy) feed likelihood ratios; the \css{} prior prevents heuristic
methods from overriding a Pearl-anchored direction. The abstention
threshold $\hat\tau=0.34$ is principled and shown to be insensitive
within a 2× range on v14 (\S\ref{sec:sensitivity}).
\item \textbf{BGP Tracer end-to-end} --- a two-phase system whose
Phase 1 is deterministic and Phase 2 produces structured HTML reports;
the full pipeline runs in $\sim$120\,s per event (\S\ref{sec:method}).
\item \textbf{A 29-event reproducible ground-truth} --- balanced across
hijack, leak, and outage labels with four strict invariants per
event; 16 rejected candidates logged with reasons (\S\ref{sec:dataset}).
\item \textbf{Open-source release} --- the released artifact
reproduces every table and figure in this paper with one shell command.
\end{enumerate}
